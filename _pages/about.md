This is the front page of my website, hosted on GitHub pages. I am a Ph.D. candidate / Teaching assistant at the University of Texas at Austin in the ecology, evolution, and animal behavior program. This website contains all of my most current contact information, research and teaching experience, and my blog! 

General Info
======
When conducting research I follow the LTOS research philosophy <- (make into a link). I primarily study freshwater fish including the live-bearers (poeciliidae), with a focus on anthropogenic influences, sexual selection, and behavioral ecology. My work has covered topics including microplastics, artificial light at night (ALAN), sexual conflict, and female preference. As a teaching assistant, I've helped develop and lead several courses listed on my teaching page. 

Science education/communication is something I feel very passionately about. If you have any inquiries or coments about my blogpost, youtube channel, or lectures, please contact me using one of my contacts on the bar to the left.
